What these numbers mean

HHI is the sum of squared partner shares: near 1 means recorded trade is concentrated in fewer partners. Effective partner count is 1 / HHI. Global supplier HHI describes world export concentration for this product, and is a different comparison level.

Keep the boundaries visible

These are positive, reconciled bilateral estimates, not individual shipments, retail prices, disruption forecasts or import dependence relative to domestic consumption. An absent record is not a proven zero. Source v is thousands of current USD; q is metric tonnes. Former Sudan736 stays separate from Sudan729.
